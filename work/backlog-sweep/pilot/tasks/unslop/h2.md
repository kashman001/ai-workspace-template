Subject: Incident Summary — Checkout Disruption On October 14th

Hi team,

I wanted to take a moment to share a comprehensive overview of the incident that impacted our checkout experience earlier this week — an event that underscores the importance of operational vigilance in today's complex infrastructure landscape.

**What Happened:** On 2026-10-14, between 09:12 and 09:58 UTC (a total of 46 minutes), checkout requests were intermittently failing, with approximately 31% of requests being affected. This was not just a technical blip, but a meaningful disruption to our customers' journey.

**Root Cause:** It was determined that the TLS certificate on the internal gateway `gw-internal-2` had expired, effectively severing trust between services and highlighting a gap in our certificate lifecycle management.

**Detection:** The issue was detected by our alerting system after 6 minutes, reflecting the strength of our monitoring — though, it could potentially be argued that there may be room for further improvement.

**Resolution:** The certificate was renewed manually by the on-call engineer, restoring service and ensuring that checkout functionality returned to normal.

**Next Steps:** Moving forward, we're committed to strengthening our resilience:
- Certificate renewal will be automated via cert-manager by November 1 — owned by Priya.
- An expiry alert will be added that fires 14 days before any certificate expires — owned by Marcus.

Experts agree that proactive certificate management is a crucial pillar of reliability, and these actions serve as a testament to our commitment to excellence. The future looks bright!
