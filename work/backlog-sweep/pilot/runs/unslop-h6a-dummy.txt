## Proposed Approach: Moving Session Storage to Postgres

Our session store is a single Redis instance with no replica, and it has become a bottleneck. When it restarted in August, roughly 18,000 users were logged out.

**Proposal:** Store sessions in a new `sessions` table in our existing Postgres cluster, which has three replicas. A nightly job would remove expired rows to keep the table small.

**Trade-offs:** Session reads are expected to rise from about 1ms to about 4ms at p99. That should be acceptable given our 200ms page budget.

**Rollout:** Roll out in three phases: dual-write for two weeks, then switch reads to Postgres, then remove Redis.

We expect this change to improve reliability.
