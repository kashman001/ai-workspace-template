## Proposed approach: moving session storage to Postgres

Our session store is a single Redis node with no replica, and it is a bottleneck. A restart in August logged out roughly 18,000 users.

We propose storing sessions in a new `sessions` table in the existing Postgres cluster, which has three replicas. A nightly job would delete expired rows to keep the table small.

The cost is latency. Session reads are expected to rise from about 1ms to about 4ms at p99, which fits within our 200ms page budget.

Rollout would happen in three steps. We dual-write for two weeks, then switch reads to Postgres, then remove Redis.

We expect this change to improve reliability.
