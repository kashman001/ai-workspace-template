## Switch invoice-sync from polling to webhooks

This changes how invoice-sync talks to the billing provider. Instead of polling, it now uses webhooks. It's the foundation for the real-time billing roadmap.

- **API calls:** Daily calls drop from roughly 12,000 to around 400.
- **Retries:** Each webhook delivery is retried up to 3 times.
- **Data model:** Migration 0042 adds a `webhook_events` table, which is the single source of truth for every incoming event.
- **Flag:** The whole feature sits behind the `INVOICE_WEBHOOKS` flag, which is off by default.

## Testing

Validated in staging over 5 days, and the team believes it's ready. To roll back, flip the flag off. No redeploy is needed.
