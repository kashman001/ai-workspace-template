Subject: Incident summary: checkout disruption on October 14

Hi team,

Earlier this week, checkout requests failed intermittently for 46 minutes. Here is what happened.

What happened. On 2026-10-14, between 09:12 and 09:58 UTC, about 31% of checkout requests failed. Customers were unable to complete purchases during that window.

Root cause. The TLS certificate on the internal gateway `gw-internal-2` expired, so services stopped trusting each other. We had no process that would have caught the expiry.

Detection. Our alerting fired after 6 minutes. That is acceptable, but we could probably make it faster.

Resolution. The on-call engineer renewed the certificate manually, and checkout returned to normal.

Next steps:
- Priya will automate certificate renewal with cert-manager by November 1.
- Marcus will add an alert that fires 14 days before any certificate expires.

Questions or thoughts are welcome.

Best,
The SRE Team
