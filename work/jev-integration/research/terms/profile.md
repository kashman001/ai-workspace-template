# Profile — `terms` (Jev / TypeSafe commercial terms), checked 2026-09-23

**One line:** Jev is priced at one public number — $42 per billion input tokens ($0.042 per million), output tokens free — published on the docs Models page, not on a pricing page (there is none); everything else commercial is either in a Master Customer Agreement dated Sep 19, 2026, announced only on the vendor's X account (open self-serve access and a $5 starting credit, 2026-09-20), or not published on any docs, legal, homepage or console page. (corrected 2026-09-23; previously: "or not published at all.")

## Price
The only price-bearing page is `https://docs.typesafe.ai/models`: `jev-1.13.0` at "$42 / $0.042" per Btok / Mtok, "Charged per input token. Output tokens are free." The homepage repeats "$42 / Per Billion input tokens." and "238x Lower input price than Claude Fable 5.1"; the launch post repeats "$0.042 / MTok ($42 per billion tokens)" and "Output tokens: FREE (too cheap to meter)"; OpenRouter resells at the same rate. `typesafe.ai/pricing` and `docs.typesafe.ai/pricing` are both 404. **The qualifier "production prices" (standing claim S4) appears on no first-party page** — the qualifiers that do exist are the launch post's "We can't prove it isn't subsidized … (which we expect to go down, not up)", the homepage FAQ answer to "Are these prices temporary or subsidized?" — "We can serve Jev profitably at our current prices. Our goal is to make intelligence more affordable over time as we improve the technology." (in the page's Framer module script, not the served HTML; corrected 2026-09-23, previously: "a homepage FAQ heading … whose answer is not in the served HTML") — and cookbook comments tagging the rate as "Historical … as of 2026-08". Enterprise/custom pricing is undisclosed (contact sales@typesafe.ai).

## Limits and auth
Rate limits are published beside the price: 250,000 tokens per second and 1,200 requests per minute for `jev-1.13.0`; exceeding either returns 429. The same page warns "Rate limits are adjusting dynamically" and "can change without notice"; higher limits only on "custom and enterprise plans". Whether the limits are per key, per account or global is not stated; no concurrency cap, tier ladder, or `x-ratelimit-*` headers are documented, and the live OpenAPI spec (`api.typesafe.ai/openapi.json`, v0.2.0) declares only 200/422 responses. Auth is an API key as `Authorization: Bearer <API_KEY>` (OpenAPI `HTTPBearer`); no `x-api-key`, no OAuth. Keys come from the login-gated console (`console.typesafe.ai/keys`); the logged-out console offers "Continue with Google" or an emailed code. Key format, rotation, scopes, org/project model, service accounts and SSO are all undocumented. Request size: 64k tokens per request, 32k for `state` plus the longest question.

## Data and privacy
Three sources say inputs are not trained on: the Privacy Policy (Nov 19, 2025) "We will not train or fine tune any … models on your prompts or other Input"; the MCA §4.1 (with a "without Customer's prior consent" carve-out); and the docs Models page. The caveat is the MCA's perpetual licence to derive **Telemetry** (logs, hashes, statistics, "learnings"), which TypeSafe "may Process … without restriction". Retention has no numeric window ("as long as reasonably necessary"; MCA: "may delete Customer Data at any time"); zero data retention is offered to enterprise customers via privacy@typesafe.ai, with no API option. Services are "hosted in the United States"; the cloud provider is not named. A public DPA (Apr 24, 2026) gives processor role, CCPA no-sell, 72-hour breach notice, annual audit right, EU SCCs Module 2/3 and the UK Addendum. SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / HIPAA are claimed nowhere readable; a Vanta trust center at `trust.typesafe.ai` exists but serves only a JS shell to non-browser fetches.

## Legal
No single "Terms of Service": the service is governed by the **Master Customer Agreement** (`typesafe.ai/legal/mca`, Sep 19, 2026); `typesafe.ai/legal/terms` is a website Terms of Use that defers product use to a separate agreement; the console login links the ToU, not the MCA. Output: TypeSafe "disclaims ownership of Output" and assigns its interest "if any" to the customer, while warning output "MAY NOT BE UNIQUE" and may be inaccurate. Integrating the API into customer applications is expressly licensed; there is **no automation or AI-agent restriction** (the ToU's anti-bot clause is website-scoped). Prohibited: resale "as a standalone service", distillation / training an imitating or competing model, reverse engineering, and any "security or vulnerability test". No high-risk-use clause and no AUP page. California law, binding JAMS arbitration without opt-out, class waiver; liability capped at the greater of 12 months' fees or $50. SDKs are MIT (npm `@typesafe-ai/sdk` 0.6.0, PyPI `typesafe-sdk` 0.7.1).

## Support and status
A Better Stack status page exists at `status.typesafe.ai` (API 99.839%, console 99.988% over 90 days as of 2026-09-23; 23 day-cells show short outages; two written-up incidents on Sep 20–21, 2026) but is linked from no first-party page. There is **no published SLA** — the MCA offers only a warranty to "perform materially as described in its Documentation", remedied by a 30-day fix or termination with refund of unused prepaid fees, and disclaims uninterrupted service. Support is "commercially reasonable efforts … in accordance with its standard support policies" (unpublished) via support@typesafe.ai, with no response-time commitment; community is a Discord server and GitHub issues. Contacts: support@, sales@, privacy@, hello@; no security@ and no security.txt. Only SDK changelogs exist; there is no product/API changelog.

## What matters for the integration decision
1. The price is real and documented, but explicitly provisional (dynamic limits, "can't prove it isn't subsidized", credits that expire; the homepage FAQ's counter-claim is "We can serve Jev profitably at our current prices") — budget on it, do not contract on it. (amended 2026-09-23, R6a: FAQ answer added beside the launch-post qualifier)
2. Access is announced as open and self-serve (X, 2026-09-20) with $5 starting credit; a third-party report says the console sign-up flow returned HTTP 500 on Sep 21–22 — verify by attempting sign-up before planning around a gate. Plan to email sales@ or privacy@ for anything enterprise (ZDR, limits, SLA). (corrected 2026-09-23; previously: "Access is not demonstrably self-serve today; expect a gate (invite/waitlist) and plan to email sales@ or privacy@ for anything enterprise (ZDR, limits, SLA).")

The vendor's own homepage FAQ ("Can Jev still get things wrong?") says it in plain words: "Yes. Jev guarantees the shape of its answers, not that every decision is correct." and tells you to use its probabilities "to set the threshold for when your software acts automatically and when it needs further review" — the FAQ module carries a second copy reading "acts autonomously" (both quoted in `record.md`'s appendix; neither adopted).
